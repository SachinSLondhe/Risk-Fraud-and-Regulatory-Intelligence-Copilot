---

### S1. Scope
#### S1.P1
This policy governs credit risk monitoring within the RegIntel Copilot demonstration. It covers credit utilization spikes and related risk indicators.

---

### S2. Indicators and Rules
#### S2.1 Sudden Credit Utilization Increase (CRD_001)
##### S2.1.P1
Monitor credit accounts where utilization exceeds 80% of the approved credit limit. Alert when utilization increases by more than 50 percentage points within a 30-day period.

##### S2.1.P2
Evidence required: current balance, credit limit, utilization percentage, recent transaction history contributing to the utilization increase, and account holder payment history.

---

### S3. Investigation Expectations
#### S3.P1
Credit utilization alerts should be investigated to determine whether the increase is consistent with the account holder's expected behavior and financial capacity.

---

### S4. Limitations
#### S4.P1
This is a synthetic demonstration policy. Actual credit risk assessment requires comprehensive analysis of borrower capacity, collateral, and market conditions as defined by applicable prudential regulations.
